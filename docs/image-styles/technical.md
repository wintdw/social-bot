Treatment: technical / drafted schematic. For engineering explainers where the
*structure* is the point (System Design, Architecture, foundation).

Draw the idea as a precise technical illustration: clean axonometric or
isometric line-work, measured and deliberate, a sense of grid, joints and load
paths. Thin confident lines on the paper ground, restrained hatching, muted
tones only. The feeling is a well-drafted schematic from an engineer's notebook —
exact, unhurried, quietly authoritative. Keep it sparse: one structure or
mechanism, lots of negative space. Not a photograph, not a UI mockup.

No people, no faces, no hands. No lettering, no numbers, no dimension figures —
the drawing carries the idea on its own. No stock icon sets, no cloud-and-arrow
clip-art, no glowing holographic screens, no circuit-board clichés.
