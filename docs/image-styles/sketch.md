Treatment: loose sketch. For first-person essays and opinion, where the writer is
thinking out loud rather than explaining.

Draw the idea as a quick hand-drawn ink sketch: a single continuous line,
slightly imperfect, playful and candid. A light wash of the palette tones, no
polish, the visible gesture of the hand. It should read as a note jotted in a
margin — honest, warm and unguarded, not a finished illustration. Keep it
simple; the roughness is the point.
