Treatment: editorial still life. The default look — contemplative, not
technical. For foundation, career and mindset ideas where the point is a
thought, not a system.

Draw the idea as a quiet still life of one or two real objects resting on the
paper ground — a folded sheet, a stack of books, a single tool, a paper plane, a
sealed envelope. Soft directional light, one clear subject, generous empty
space, a gentle cast shadow. The objects are a visual metaphor for the idea,
arranged simply and deliberately. Warm, calm, human: the mood of a thought being
turned over.
