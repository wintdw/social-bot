Treatment: bulletin / poster. For announcements, events, community posts and
news, where the message should read at a glance.

Draw the idea as a clean minimal poster composition: a few bold flat shapes from
the palette, a strong simple silhouette, one clear focal element, centred and
calm. Flat colour only, crisp edges, generous margins. Celebratory but
restrained — an announcement that feels considered, not loud. No confetti
clutter, no busy detail.
