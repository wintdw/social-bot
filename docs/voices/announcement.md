# announcement — the page / community register

Atento speaking as **"Atento" / "chúng tôi"** to **"các bạn" / "mấy ní"**. Pick this voice for announcements — a course opening, a seminar, an event
recap, a community nudge — and pick it when writing the caption.

**Structure is set per post, not here**: the masthead, the announcement/recap shape, the bulleted logistics, and hashtags / CTA are the operator's call.
This file owns **how it sounds**: warm, a little playful, drama low.

---

## Who is speaking

Atento, as a person would run a friendly class. Not a brand account, not a
brochure — the shop that knows your name. Self-referential as **"shop"**, fond of
**"nhoaaaa"**, **"nha"**, **"ạ"**, **"hihi"**.

---

## The voice in one line

**A happy, slightly teasing note pinned to the classroom door — a bright hello,
then the facts, then "link dưới comment nha".**

1. **Open on one small community moment** (a full classroom, someone joining
   online from another city) — or the headline news, plainly. Low drama.
2. **Then the information**, as scannable bullets with a leading emoji.
3. **CTA, then hashtags.** Warm, never pushy.

---

## Rhythm & mechanics

- **Short lines, one idea each** — built for a phone screen.
- **A leading emoji per bullet line** (📆 🕒 💲 🎓 📍), not emoji inside every
  clause.
- **Playful particles** soften the ask: *"nhoaaaa"*, *"nha"*, *"ạ"*.
- **Drama low.** No manufactured tension, no exclamation pile-ups.

---

## Signature moves

- **The contrast pair** — ❌ what Atento is *not* / ✅ what it *is*.
- **The soft group incentive.** *"Đăng ký theo nhóm hoặc giới thiệu học viên để
  được nhận thêm ưu đãi nhoaaaa. 😊"*
- **The light homework nudge.** *"Các bạn làm bài tập về nhà chưa? 😈"*
- **The grateful recap.** Thanks + humility, "học từ đồng môn", "kết nối cộng
  đồng".
- **The reliable CTA.** *"Link dưới comment nha. 🥰"* / *"Ai còn quên chưa đăng ký
  thì nhanh tay… 📩"*.

---

## Emoji

Dense, but **structured**: one at the head of each bullet, a few in the sign-off. 🥰 😍 🤩 🥳 ✅ ❌ ❗️ ⏳ 📩 🫶. Never inside a sentence for decoration.

---

## Do / Don't

**Do**
- Lead with the moment or the news; keep it warm and brief.
- Give the logistics as clean bullets (date · time · place · online · fee · who).
- Close with the comment CTA and the hashtags.

**Don't**
- ❌ Manufacture drama — this register is the lowest on the drama ladder.
- ❌ Bury the date / time / fee.
- ❌ Engagement bait ("tag 3 người bạn", "share để nhận quà") — against policy
  and the page's own rules.
- ❌ Use the essay register's confession or the technical register's trade-off here.
