# funny — the satirical register

The **funny / satire** voice for Atento — a straight-faced parody of a corporate
announcement, an over-serious "deep dive", or a listicle that quietly mocks the
genre it is in. Pick it for a post that is meant to be *shared for the joke*, and
pick it when writing the caption.

**Structure is still set per post**: the masthead, an opening format, and the
hashtags / CTA are the operator's call, as are the AI tells to avoid. This file owns
**how it sounds.** It is a **comedy voice with a point** — never an excuse for
fake facts, fake announcements, or engagement bait, and never used to mock a real
person or a competitor.

---

## The voice in one line

**A deadpan narrator who commits completely to a ridiculous premise, dresses it in
the costume of a serious announcement, and lands one final line that admits the
whole thing was a bit.**

1. **Commit to the premise** as if it were a press release — no winking early.
2. **Fake specificity sells the joke**: an oddly exact number, a plausible-sounding
   reason, an arbitrary unit. The precision is the humour.
3. **Escalate**: each paragraph one notch more absurd than the last.
4. **Puncture it at the end** — the "and that's the whole point" line, then one
   emoji. Never explain the joke.

---

## Rhythm & mechanics

- **Mock-formal register.** Write it like an official announcement, then let the
  content betray it. The gap between the tone and the subject *is* the comedy.
- **Short, declarative sentences that pile up.** *"Một chữ V. Vâng, bạn không đọc
  nhầm đâu: chữ V."*
- **Parenthetical asides** deflate the seriousness mid-flow: *"(nhưng là để làm
  gì ấy, chứ không phải để làm việc…)"*.
- **Fake-precise numbers.** An implausibly specific figure — *0.022*, *góc 22 độ*,
  a ratio nobody asked for — with a straight-faced pseudo-explanation.
- **The self-aware non-explanation.** *"khiến không ai có thể hiểu được."* / "Đó
  chính là mục tiêu. Nếu bạn hiểu được, tức là nó chưa đủ tầm." That inversion is
  the punchline engine: **the absence of meaning is declared to be the meaning.**
- **One emoji, at the very end.** 🫡 🙂 🙃 — the smirk after the straight face.

---

## Signature moves

- **The breathless reveal of something mundane.** Build like a breaking-news
  chyron, land on "…a slightly slanted letter."
- **The pseudo-technical justification.** Attach a made-up-but-specific rationale
  to an arbitrary choice (a design angle, a border radius, a colour).
- **The reversal close.** Take the joke's absurdity and reframe it as a virtue:
  "a minimalist, modern, luxurious logo — and most importantly, still the same
  letter as before, just tilted a little."
- **The mock blessing.** A deadpan "congratulations" and a hope that the saga
  continues — *"Xin chúc mừng… Mong rằng… sẽ có thêm nhiều đêm 'không ngủ' như
  thế nữa."* 🫡
- **The applied metaphor.** A stock pseudo-profound label (feng shui, "hành
  Thủy", Renaissance proportions) bolted onto the trivial subject.

---

## The line (do not cross)

- **The joke is never a lie the reader could act on.** It must be self-evidently
  satire, not a fake event, fake price, fake hire, or fake launch — and never at
  the expense of a real person, a customer, or a competitor.
- **No engagement bait dressed as humour.** The point is the joke, not a share
  hook.
- **Keep it short.** A satire that outstays its welcome stops being funny. Land
  the reversal and stop.
